PROJECT 01

CYBERLUCID

Cybersecurity made clear for everyone.

CyberLucid is a free, beginner-friendly cybersecurity education platform designed for everyday people — not just cybersecurity professionals. No technical background, account, or subscription is required. The goal is simple: help people learn, recognize threats, protect themselves, practice their knowledge, explore new threats, and ask questions.

Learn

Introduces cybersecurity fundamentals and explains why everyday users are targeted and how attackers exploit trust, fear, urgency, curiosity, and authority.

Recognize

Covers phishing, malware, ransomware, social engineering, fake websites, deepfakes, QR scams, account takeover, SIM swapping, and other common threats.

Protect

Provides practical guidance for passwords, MFA, account recovery, device security, backups, suspicious links, Wi-Fi, and personal information.

Lucid Analyzer

Helps users examine suspicious messages, emails, and links while explaining the warning signs instead of simply returning a "SCAM" or "SAFE" label.

Threat Explorer

An interactive environment for discovering threats beyond the common attacks users already know.

Practice & Ask

Flashcards, quizzes, and LucidBot help users reinforce concepts and ask cybersecurity questions in plain language.

PROJECT 02

AI Drone Flight Agent

Natural-language autonomous flight control

A multi-agent AI system that allows a simulated aircraft to be controlled using plain English. Instead of manually programming every waypoint, an operator can describe a desired flight and the system interprets the request, plans the mission, validates it for safety, converts it into executable MAVLink commands, and carries it out inside an ArduPlane SITL environment.

Natural Language Control

Converts instructions such as "Survey camp a then camp b and return home" into structured flight actions.

Mission Planning

Breaks complex requests into smaller executable steps such as takeoff, navigation, loitering, approach, and landing.

Safety Validation

Validates missions against configured altitude, airspeed, landing-point, and known-hazard constraints before execution.

Fixed-Wing Flight

Designed around a simulated fixed-wing aircraft rather than a hovering quadcopter, using waypoints, orbit patterns, and runway-aligned landing sequences.

Flight Patterns

Supports racetrack patterns, figure-eight patterns, and lawnmower survey patterns generated from waypoint geometry.

Learning Machine

Can learn operator preferences such as preferred altitude, airspeed, locations, and recurring mission patterns.

Obstacle Avoidance

Supports short flight detours that allow the aircraft to offset around an obstacle and automatically rejoin the active mission.

Runway Landing

Builds runway-aligned approaches using configured runway endpoints rather than simply sending the aircraft to a single landing point.

Simulation First

Runs inside ArduPilot SITL, allowing experimentation without requiring a physical aircraft.
